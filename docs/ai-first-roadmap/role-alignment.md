# First-party AI role sample (checked 27 October 2026)

This is a dated sample of first-party career pages across major product and AI
companies. It is not a complete list of roles, does not assert that every
posting remains open, and does not promise a hiring outcome.

| Company | Example role/page | Observable emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenAI | [Applied AI Engineer, Singapore](https://openai.com/careers/applied-ai-engineer-singapore/) | Customer use-case selection through evaluation, production launch, scale, and measurable business outcomes. |
| Anthropic | [Careers listings](https://www.anthropic.com/careers/jobs) and [Applied AI Engineer, Enterprise](https://www.anthropic.com/careers/jobs/5390795008) | Production systems for real users, enterprise integration, and customer-facing technical delivery. |
| Google | [Forward Deployed Engineer III, GenAI](https://careers.google.com/jobs/results/127965694384841414/) | Embedded building, debugging and shipping agentic AI in customer environments. |
| NVIDIA | [Applied AI Engineer](https://jobs.nvidia.com/careers/job/893396754150) | End-to-end ownership of an AI agent, LLM-powered workflow, or intelligent automation system. |
| Amazon | [Principal Applied AI Solutions Architect](https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10584832/principal-applied-ai-solutions-architect-amazon-connect) | Data readiness, governance and customer AI-agent/RAG deployments. |
| Microsoft | [AI and Data careers, Mumbai](https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/locations/mumbai.html) | Reusable enterprise AI solution patterns, customer scale, and technical leadership. |
| Meta | [Official careers search](https://www.metacareers.com/jobs) | Search portal included in the scan; no specific role claim is made from the search landing page. |

Across the specific sampled postings, recurring capabilities include building
real AI systems, model and retrieval evaluation, safe integration with
enterprise or developer workflows, and explaining measured outcomes. These
are project-planning signals only. They do not replace general software
knowledge, DSA, independent work experience, customer ownership, or company-
specific interview preparation.
