# Sprint 5 interview sessions and engineering tasks

Sprint 5 covers the existing Days 57–70 (19 December 2026–1 January 2027). Each day remains 120 engineering minutes: 25 study, 85 practice/testing, and 10 evidence. This schedule adds no hours.

Four 45-minute interview mocks occur twice in each seven-day practice week: Days 58 and 60, then Days 66 and 69. Each replaces 45 minutes of the existing 85-minute practice allocation and leaves a bounded 40-minute engineering task (5 minutes preconditions/prediction, 25 minutes implementation or observation, 10 minutes selector/result/blocker record). The mock phases are exactly Requirements (5), Capacity and constraints (5), Initial architecture and data model (15), Bottlenecks, reliability, scaling (10), and Trade-offs, failures, follow-ups (10). The learner saves a proposal before hints, references, worked answers, or AI. Use the existing 100-point [mock rubric](mock-rubric.md).

The remaining ten days use a task-specific five-minute prediction before guidance, then retain 80 minutes for the existing acceptance. Day 63 uses that five minutes to identify the evidence-based first recovery blocker; Day 70 uses 5 + up to 50 recovery + its existing 20-minute handover/mock + 10-minute triage. Neither recovery day gains work or overtime. Day 70 is not a fifth 45-minute interview mock.

All original acceptance stays authoritative. Every open portion remains named in the day's `deferred_work_id`; a mock, proposal, instructor reference, or scaffold check does not award completion.
