# Day 121 — 2027-02-23: Report uncertain outcomes honestly

## Scope

Treat a lost reply as unknown until fresh inspection. Preserve original receipt/ack evidence and surface database-unavailable as unknown.

**Assistant-provided architecture (what and why):** [Day 121 note](../assistant-architecture-notes.md#day-121). The assistant supplies the architecture; implement and test the named seam. System-design practice is only the separate interview question set; no capstone proposal or post-hoc design justification is assigned.

- Study: 25 minutes total; one shared cap.
- Engineering: 85 minutes implementation/testing + 10 minutes evidence.
- No exact course lesson is assigned; use the worked example and focused public reading.

### Worked trace

Simulate the reply being lost after the database commit callback: the receipt is present but acknowledgment is still NULL. Snapshot receipt payload/time and ack before reinspection; reconcile and prove the original receipt is unchanged. A deliberately unreachable database must propagate an unknown/error observation, never ABSENT. Through the actual retry service also reject acknowledged-without-receipt and conflicting-receipt evidence without mutation.

Focused reading: [PostgreSQL 18 transactions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/tutorial-transactions.html) — reading focus: Commit outcome and lost caller response are different observations.
