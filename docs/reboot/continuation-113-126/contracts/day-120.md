### Worked trace

A matching receipt with no ack skips receipt insertion and commits ack separately. An absent receipt creates one receipt commit, then ack commit; failure never triggers an in-process retry. The test queries pg_backend_pid() through the transaction-bound connection and through a raw independent DataSource connection after receipt commit, and proves the latter sees the receipt before acknowledgment. Injected acknowledgment failure leaves that receipt committed and pending for later reconciliation.

Focused reading: [Spring programmatic transactions](https://docs.spring.io/spring-framework/reference/data-access/transaction/programmatic.html) — reading focus: Keep receipt and acknowledgement as separately committed operations.
