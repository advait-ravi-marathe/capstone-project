A consolidation day has no new mandatory problem. Spend 20 minutes cold-solving the weakest due anchor and 15 minutes actively recalling/logging the other due anchors. A failed recall displaces the next new anchor; never add overtime. If a due review cannot fit, record the problem and original due date, carry it visibly to the next consolidation slot, and displace new work as needed. Never silently defer a review or mark it complete without evidence. A planned due date is not evidence of a completed review. Compute each due date from the actual first-attempt date, not the roadmap date. All reviews remain inside the same 60-minute DSA block.
