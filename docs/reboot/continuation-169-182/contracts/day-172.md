## Practice

Sort candidates by descending distinct-positive-viewer count, then exact media ID ascending; take at most three. The same list is used for every viewer, including already-seen items. Extra time goes to tie and short-list tests.

## Worked example

Scores d=3, b=2, c=1, a=0 produce [d, b, c]. Equal scores must ignore input order. Two candidates produce two recommendations, not padded duplicates.
