### The effect-before-acknowledgment crash window

Require a receipt before ack. If ack fails, keep receipt and pending work visible for later manual recovery.

Focused source: [AWS transactional outbox pattern](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/transactional-outbox.html).
