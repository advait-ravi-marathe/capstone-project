### Map the captured event, not today’s mutable policy

Use a JDBC RowMapper; do not reconstruct historical payload from mutable policy state. Preserve deterministic order and fail on unsupported values.

Focused source: [Spring JDBC core reference](https://docs.spring.io/spring-framework/reference/data-access/jdbc/core.html).
