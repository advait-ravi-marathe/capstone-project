# AIFDE-V2-U027: Discovery, scope change and handoff draft

**Learner status: Not started.** Scheduled Days36–37 (60-minute primary +60-minute support/buffer). This is a bounded role-play or authorized peer exercise; external outreach is not implied.

## Learn and do

Use a CoverStory scenario to ask about the user's task, source authority, access boundary, answer failure cost, current workaround, and a measurable success criterion. Mark every answer as `role_play`, `authorized_peer`, or `actual_user`; never blend hypothetical statements into real customer feedback. Choose one scope change and draft a plain-language handoff.

Use [the role bank](../role-bank.md) for the 30-minute interview block; it remains separate from the 90-minute AI block. Copy the template below to your private evidence directory and fill only evidence actually gathered:

```text
docs/fde-v2/templates/u027-discovery-handoff.md
```

No organization or personal data is needed; synthetic scenario content is sufficient for practice.

## Acceptance and evidence

Include baseline/workaround, one proposed success measure, non-goals, data restrictions, a go/no-go limit, one tradeoff, and evidence-source labels. A role-play cannot be reported as customer discovery or adoption. The instructor checks the required fields and allowed evidence labels; that structural check does not establish stakeholder validation.
