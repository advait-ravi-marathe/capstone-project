## Stage sequence

### M53-S01 — Capability evidence inventory

**Worked example and learner task:** Separate scaffold, learner attempt, local model, hosted provider, live cloud, real user, production and organizational-impact evidence.

**Acceptance/evidence:** Every capability claim needs a hashed source receipt and actual contributor.

### M53-S02 — Primary case-study draft

**Worked example and learner task:** Describe actual problem, unchanged baseline, personal change, evaluation, failure and reproducibility. Use only verified measurements.

**Acceptance/evidence:** No invented adoption, savings, scale or customer identity; draft is not publication.

### M53-S03 — Bounded-tools/MCP case

**Worked example and learner task:** Explain actual protocol/tool exchange, exact-action approval, one denial/replay and local scope.

**Acceptance/evidence:** No enterprise OAuth or customer automation claim from local stdio.

### M53-S04 — Role-fit evidence matrix

**Worked example and learner task:** Use dated official role descriptions. Map technical requirements to verified receipts; mark location/work authorization/travel and experience unknowns explicitly.

**Acceptance/evidence:** No application is sent; do not infer work authorization or years of customer production.

### M53-S05 — Behavioral ownership rehearsal

**Worked example and learner task:** Prepare stories from actual experience and distinguish personal contribution from assistant/team/tutorial work.

**Acceptance/evidence:** No invented colleagues, reviewers or professional impact.

### M53-S06 — AWS practical readiness map

**Worked example and learner task:** Compare current official exam syllabus with existing gap map/lab evidence; do not repeat diagnostic.

**Acceptance/evidence:** Study hours do not imply exam readiness.

### M53-S07 — Conditional certification decision

**Worked example and learner task:** Choose pursue later/defer/not useful based on evidence and relevance. Check current costs/terms only if booking is separately requested.

**Acceptance/evidence:** No exam purchase/booking; AIP remains later experience/readiness target.

### M53-S08 — Reproduction and claims audit

**Worked example and learner task:** Re-run scoped demo and map every statement to receipts; preserve blocked/not-run status.

**Acceptance/evidence:** No private links/data shared without separate authorization.

### M53-S09 — Partial readiness and next gap

**Worked example and learner task:** Name remaining capability blockers and the smallest repair.

**Acceptance/evidence:** No universal “FDE ready” claim; elapsed 270 days or merged scaffolds cannot clear gates.

### M53-S10 — Private handoff controls

**Worked example and learner task:** Prepare private evidence index and external-action status.

**Acceptance/evidence:** Applications, outreach, exam spending and public posting need applicable separate authorization.
