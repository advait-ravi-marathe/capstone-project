## North Star

> **Become a trusted AI engineer who independently builds, evaluates and safely operates useful enterprise AI systems with verified customer outcomes.**

The first major career outcome is a suitable **job switch**. Durable AI/FDE depth continues afterward. Staff-level capability is a longer-term aspiration requiring real scope and impact—not a promised title, absolute mastery or irreplaceability.

The hierarchy is **North Star → objectives → measurable key results → existing milestones → existing sprints/daily slots**. It is an accountability overlay, not a second roadmap. Independent ownership, useful quality, enforced safety, real operations and honest user outcomes are the North Star evidence; we do not collapse them into a misleading “mastery percentage.”
