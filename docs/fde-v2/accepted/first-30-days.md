## Exact role/certification outputs

- **Day 1:** Use verified current FE/full-stack strengths and an actual target role; identify gaps and accurate CV evidence. Applications need not wait for AI work.
- **Day 2:** Explain closures/event loop/promises with one short runnable example; record a weak point.
- **Day 3:** Diagnose a state/render/key bug and explain the minimal fix.
- **Day 4:** Trace an HTTP request, caching/error behavior and one browser performance issue.
- **Day 5:** Verify the purchased course exact title/code/update if accessible; map it to the current official SAA-C03 guide. If unknown, record unknown and use the official guide; no purchase or completion claim.
- **Day 6:** Explain principals, policies, roles and least privilege through one short design scenario; record actual gaps, without provisioning resources.
- **Day 7:** Practice one genuine incident/ownership story plus follow-up questions; distinguish own contribution and actual results. Re-estimate AI scope inside the weekly evidence note.
- **Day 8:** Explain effect/dependency/cancellation or stale-state behavior using a small code path.
- **Day 9:** Design/test loading/error/keyboard behavior for one component.
- **Day 10:** Scope a workflow; choose state/data caching boundaries and explain tradeoffs.
- **Day 11:** Design validation, status/error contract, auth boundary and idempotency for one endpoint.
- **Day 12:** Explain VPC/subnet/routing/security-group boundaries for the app; classify uncertainty and review the relevant official syllabus.
- **Day 13:** Choose storage, encryption, backup and data-access controls for one scenario; explain alternatives and costs conceptually, not as measured billing.
- **Day 14:** Use 20 min diagnosis + 10 min review; log root cause and missing knowledge.
- **Day 15:** Ask requirements first; make explicit traffic/data assumptions and choose a simple architecture.
- **Day 16:** Walk through cache/queue/storage/failure decisions for the same scoped system.
- **Day 17:** Model a small domain with clear invariants/interfaces; use patterns only where they solve a problem.
- **Day 18:** Explain a race, retry or idempotency problem and a tested remedy.
- **Day 19:** Compare the relevant compute/deployment options and recovery/availability choices for CoverStory; no deployment or spend implied.
- **Day 20:** Explain logs, metrics, secrets, quotas, billing alerts and teardown; state that budget alerts are not a guaranteed hard cost cap.
- **Day 21:** Use 20 min unseen target-role prompt + 10 min scoring; repair the weakest observed dimension.
- **Day 22:** Make a small requirements-driven component/data transformation without a supplied solution.
- **Day 23:** Explain measurement, profiling and one justified optimization; avoid premature memoization claims.
- **Day 24:** Review one auth/access/data boundary and an integration failure path.
- **Day 25:** Give a five-minute design explanation and answer follow-up tradeoffs within the 30 min block.
- **Day 26:** Use lawful current official sample questions or an authorized assessment; record domain errors and reasoning. A short sample is not a full-length readiness pass.
- **Day 27:** Map remaining SAA-C03 knowledge/practical gaps to planned AWS project work; decide next study topics, not an invented exam/pass date.
- **Day 28:** Time-box one original task and score it; a 30 min segment is not falsely called a full employer loop.
- **Day 29:** Reattempt the weakest prior topic with a fresh prompt; no arbitrary topic-volume quota.
- **Day 30:** Review actual interview evidence, permitted application preparation, AI carryover and next-month priorities; no automatic outreach.

AWS project/lab work may cover a certification domain, but its minutes are counted only once in AI time. No lab, cloud charge, course purchase or exam booking is authorized by this schedule. Azure/Google certification or deployment tracks are not active scope.

**Day 30 boundary:** the U023 30-minute segment creates adversarial fixtures and expected deterministic-boundary notes only. Execution and regression evidence continue onDay 31. Neither U023 nor the P1 release is required or claimed complete onDay 30.
