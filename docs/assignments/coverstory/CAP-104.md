## Acceptance and design boundary

The tests cover: event time 10:04 arriving at 10:06 revises 2/1 to 3/2 with zero effects; replaying that ID leaves 3/2 and counts one duplicate; 10:08 is quarantined with the old summary intact; exactly 10:07 is accepted while 10:07:00.000001 is too late; and UNKNOWN evidence routes to reconciliation without a new charge or mutation of payment authority.

Analytics grace determines whether a summary revision is accepted. It does not expire authoritative payment evidence. A late callback must remain available to the existing reconciliation workflow even when its analytics observation is quarantined. Summary recomputation cannot replay capture, refund, or insurer dispatch.

The instructor reference evaluator is separate from the learner seam and rejects an exclusive-boundary mutant. No live provider, payment database, consumer data, or deployment is used by these tests. The broader frontend, security, and runtime prerequisites remain governed by the starter setup; this assignment adds no frontend flow.
