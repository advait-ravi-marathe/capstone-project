## Evidence and status rules

- **READY** means the instructor setup for the saved contract has executable evidence. It does not mean learner completion or a measured day-length timebox.
- **PARTIAL** means some saved-contract setup is runnable, but a specific saved product boundary, dependency, artifact, or rehearsal remains absent. The matrix's `coverage_note` is the exact current boundary when present.
- **MAPPED ONLY** means the source card and its guide are available but no authored family scaffold is recorded.
- Learner TODO logic remains unchanged. Learner acceptance runs are separate from instructor checks; zero assignments are marked complete or mastered.
